Tart cherry extract

10:1 extract per capsule. 2 capsules per serving, 30 servings.

What it is

Montmorency tart cherry, concentrated 10:1 into a 500 mg capsule. Tart cherries naturally contain small amounts of melatonin, the molecule your body makes as night falls.

Tart cherry supports the body's own melatonin and supports restful sleep.

The evidence

In a randomized placebo controlled crossover trial, 20 adults drank tart cherry juice concentrate daily for 7 days. Their measured melatonin output rose, and they slept longer with better sleep efficiency than on placebo.

Read the study: Howatson et al. 2012, European Journal of Nutrition

Why grade C+, the honest gap

The trial used juice concentrate, a large daily amount of whole fruit. Our serving is 1 g of a 10:1 extract, the rough equivalent of 10 g of cherries, which is far less and a different form. No one has run the same trial on capsules like ours. Human data exists, but not for this form at this dose. That is exactly what C+ means, and why this page will not claim more.

How to take it

2 capsules with your evening meal.
