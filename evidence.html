The grading rules

Marketing rounds up. We grade instead.

Every product we sell carries a grade on the front. The grade describes the evidence for the ingredient at the dose in the capsule, not the ingredient in general. Here is the whole system.

The grades

A

Multiple independent human randomized controlled trials at this dose.

The bar is deliberately high: independent means different research groups, not one lab repeating itself. No product we sell holds this grade. If one earns it, the label will change.

B

Human randomized controlled trials, with limitations we disclose.

Small samples, different study populations, different salts or doses, or self reported outcomes. Each product page names its specific limitation. Nothing is hidden in a footnote.

C+

Human trials exist, but of a different form or dose than what is in the capsule.

Close, not proof. The gap between what was studied and what we sell is spelled out on the product page.

C

Mechanistic or indirect evidence only.

Laboratory or animal findings that make the idea plausible, with no human trial of the ingredient alone for sleep. We still sell one grade C product, apigenin, and its page says all of this plainly.

Current grades

  • B Magnesium bisglycinate, 200 mg elemental
  • B Glycine, 3 g
  • B L-theanine, 200 mg
  • C+ Tart cherry extract, 500 mg 10:1
  • C Apigenin, 50 mg

Rules we hold ourselves to

  • Every evidence claim on this site links to the study it comes from. If we cannot link it, we do not say it.
  • Grades move when evidence moves, in both directions. A downgrade gets announced as visibly as an upgrade.
  • We name the gaps ourselves: sample size, study population, form, dose. You should not need to dig for them.
  • No grade is for sale, including our own. The grade describes the evidence, not the margin.
