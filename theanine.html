L-theanine

200mg

Per capsule. 1 capsule per serving, 30 servings.

What it is

An amino acid found in green tea, isolated. One capsule contains 200 mg, the amount used in most studies and far more than a cup of tea provides.

L-theanine supports relaxation without drowsiness during the day, and it supports restful sleep at night.

The evidence

In a randomized placebo controlled crossover trial, 30 healthy adults took 200 mg of L-theanine daily for four weeks. Their sleep quality scores improved compared to placebo, including how quickly they fell asleep and how often their sleep was disturbed. Sleep was measured by questionnaire, not by lab recording.

Read the study: Hidese et al. 2019, Nutrients

Why grade B

A proper randomized trial at our exact dose. But it was small, 30 people, and sleep was self reported, one outcome among several the study measured. That keeps it at B.

How to take it

1 capsule in the evening, with or without food. Some people take it earlier on demanding days. For sleep, take it without caffeine, for obvious reasons.

Who this is not for

  • People on blood pressure medication, without asking a doctor first. L-theanine may nudge blood pressure, and your doctor should know what you take.
  • People whose real problem is four coffees after lunch. Cutting afternoon caffeine is cheaper and does more.
  • Anyone pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or on prescription medication, without a doctor's OK first.
