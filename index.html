What the grades mean

A
Multiple independent human randomized trials at this dose. We sell nothing at this grade yet.
B
Human randomized trials, with limitations we disclose: small samples, different populations, or self reported outcomes.
C+
Human trials exist, but of a different form or dose than what is in the capsule. Close, not proof.
C
Mechanistic or indirect evidence only. No human trial of the ingredient alone for sleep.

Who this is not for

  • You want certainty. Effects in studies were modest and many participants noticed little. We do not promise results and we will not pretend otherwise.
  • You gasp, choke, or stop breathing in your sleep, or someone told you that you do. See a doctor first. That is not a supplement problem.
  • Your evenings run on caffeine, alcohol, or a bright screen at 1 am. Fix those first. They are free and they work better than anything we sell.
  • You are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or take prescription medication. Talk to your doctor before taking anything we sell.
  • You expect this to replace medical care. It does not and it cannot. If poor sleep is wrecking your days, a doctor is the right first stop.
