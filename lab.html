Each production batch goes to an independent lab before it goes to you. The certificate covers four things: identity (is it what the label says), potency (how much), heavy metals, and microbiology. The PDF for each batch is posted here, matched to the batch number printed on your unit.

The rule behind this page

No certificate, no sale. If a batch certificate is late or fails, the product goes out of stock rather than out unverified. A supplement brand asking for your trust should be able to show its homework, so here it is, or here it will be, batch by batch.
