Apigenin

50mg

Per capsule. 1 capsule per serving, 30 servings.

What it is

A flavonoid found in chamomile. One capsule contains 50 mg, far above the few milligrams in a cup of chamomile tea.

Apigenin may support relaxation. We phrase it that carefully because the evidence is grade C, and this page explains what that means.

The evidence

In laboratory studies, apigenin binds to GABA-A receptors, part of the brain's calming signaling, and animal studies show quieting effects at high doses. No published randomized human trial has tested isolated apigenin for sleep. That sentence is the most important one on this page.

Read the receptor study: Viola et al. 1995, Planta Medica

Why grade C

Grade C means mechanistic evidence only. We sell apigenin because the mechanism is plausible and people ask for it. We grade it C because that is what the evidence supports. If you want human trial evidence behind every purchase, buy something else on this site. If a real trial is published, this grade moves and we will link it here.

How to take it

1 capsule 30 to 60 minutes before bed.

Who this is not for

  • Anyone who wants human trial evidence behind every product. Apigenin does not have it yet, and we will not pretend otherwise.
  • People on daily medication, without asking a doctor or pharmacist first. Apigenin can interact with how the body processes some medicines.
  • Anyone pregnant, breastfeeding, or under 18.
