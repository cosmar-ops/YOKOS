Magnesium bisglycinate

200mg

Elemental magnesium per serving. 2 capsules, 30 servings.

What it is

Magnesium bound to two molecules of glycine. This form is gentle on digestion and well absorbed. Each serving contains 200 mg of elemental magnesium, meaning the mineral itself, not the weight of the whole compound. Many labels blur that distinction. Ours does not.

Magnesium supports normal nervous system function and muscle relaxation, and it supports restful sleep.

The evidence

In a randomized placebo controlled trial, 46 older adults with poor sleep took 500 mg of magnesium daily for 8 weeks. The magnesium group fell asleep faster, spent more of their time in bed asleep, and scored better on sleep questionnaires than the placebo group. Total sleep time was not significantly different between groups. We tell you both parts.

Read the study: Abbasi et al. 2012, Journal of Research in Medical Sciences

A newer randomized placebo controlled trial tested magnesium bisglycinate itself, our exact form, in healthy adults reporting poor sleep. Read it before you buy.

Read the study: magnesium bisglycinate randomized trial, Nature and Science of Sleep

Why grade B

Human randomized trials exist, but the best known one used a different salt and a higher dose than ours, in older adults. We disclose that instead of hiding it. If independent trials at our exact dose stack up, the grade moves.

How to take it

2 capsules with water, 1 to 2 hours before bed. With or without food. If your stools loosen, drop to 1 capsule.

Who this is not for

  • People with kidney problems, unless a doctor says otherwise. The kidneys clear magnesium, and yours should be working well before you supplement it.
  • People who already eat plenty of legumes, nuts, seeds, and whole grains. You may be getting enough magnesium from food, which costs less than we do.
  • Anyone pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or on prescription medication, without a doctor's OK first.
